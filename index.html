SkillBridge

A platform that connects university students for peer-to-peer skill exchange, enabling them to learn and monetize micro-skills in a flexible and affordable manner

Features

  • 📝

    Micro-skill posting

    Allowing users to create and publish listings for their micro-skills, including descriptions, pricing, and availability

  • 🔍

    Search and filtering

    Enabling users to search for and filter micro-skills by category, keyword, and location

  • 📈

    Booking and payment system

    Facilitating secure and seamless booking and payment for sessions between users

  • 📊

    Review and rating system

    Allowing users to leave reviews and ratings for each other after completing sessions, promoting accountability and quality

  • 📁

    User profiles and dashboard

    Providing users with a dedicated profile and dashboard to manage their micro-skills, bookings, and earnings
