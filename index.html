FoodRescue

Connect with local restaurants to purchase unsold food at a discount, reducing food waste and supporting the local community.

Features

  • 📈 Restaurant Onboarding: Streamlined process for restaurants to create a profile, list menu items, and set discount prices
  • 🍴 Menu Item Listing: User-friendly interface for restaurants to list and manage their unsold food items, including descriptions, prices, and images
  • 🤖 Discount Pricing Algorithm: Automated system to suggest optimal discount prices for restaurants based on factors like food type, quantity, and expiration date
  • 🛍️ Customer Purchase Flow: Intuitive and secure checkout process for customers to browse, select, and pay for discounted food items
  • 📦 Order Management System: Real-time tracking and management of customer orders, including notifications, updates, and reviews
