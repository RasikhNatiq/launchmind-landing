SkillBridge

Peer-to-peer skill exchange platform connecting university students for 1-on-1 paid sessions, providing affordable and personalized learning opportunities.

Features

  • 📝

    Micro-skill posting

    Allow users to create and publish micro-skills they can offer, including descriptions, tags, and pricing

  • 🔍

    Skill search and filtering

    Enable users to search for specific micro-skills, filter by category, price, and location, and view detailed profiles of skill providers

  • 📅

    Session scheduling and booking

    Allow users to book and schedule 1-on-1 sessions with skill providers, including calendar integration and reminders

  • 💸

    Payment processing and transaction management

    Integrate a secure payment system to facilitate transactions between users, including payment tracking and dispute resolution

  • 👍

    Review and rating system

    Enable users to leave reviews and ratings for skill providers, promoting accountability and quality control within the platform
