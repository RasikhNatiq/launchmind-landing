SkillBridge

Connect with university students for peer-to-peer skill exchange, enabling you to learn and monetize micro-skills in a personalized and affordable way

Features

  • 📚

    Skill Listing

    A searchable directory of micro-skills offered by university students, with filtering and sorting options

  • 📅

    Booking System

    A calendar-based system for students to book 1-on-1 paid sessions with each other, with reminders and notifications

  • 💸

    Payment Processing

    A secure and convenient payment gateway for students to pay for sessions, with options for refunds and disputes

  • 👥

    User Profiles

    Customizable profiles for students to showcase their skills, experience, and reviews, with options for profile verification

  • 📝

    Review Mechanism

    A rating and review system for students to evaluate their experiences with each other, with options for feedback and improvement
