SkillBridge

A platform that enables university students to exchange micro-skills with each other through paid 1-on-1 sessions, providing an affordable and personalized alternative to traditional tutoring and online courses.

Features

  • 📝

    Micro-skill posting

    Allow users to create and post micro-skills they can offer, including descriptions, categories, and pricing

  • 📅

    Session booking and payment

    Enable users to book and pay for 1-on-1 sessions with other users, including secure payment processing and scheduling

  • 👥

    User profiles and skill categorization

    Create user profiles that showcase their posted micro-skills and allow for categorization and filtering by skill type

  • 📊

    Review and rating system

    Implement a system for users to leave reviews and ratings for each other after completing sessions, to build trust and accountability within the community

  • 💬

    In-app messaging

    Provide a messaging system for users to communicate with each other before and after booking sessions, to discuss details and provide feedback
